About Us
Uganda's Trusted Safari Specialists

Ngoni Safaris Uganda is a premier tour operator offering unforgettable gorilla trekking, wildlife safaris, and nature experiences across East Africa. Our mission is to deliver high-quality, sustainable travel that satisfies our customers while respecting the social, cultural, and environmental reality of Africa.

With over a decade of experience, our expert team — fluent in multiple languages — crafts personalised itineraries that connect travellers with the real Africa: from the silverback gorillas of Bwindi Impenetrable Forest to the savannah herds of Queen Elizabeth National Park.
